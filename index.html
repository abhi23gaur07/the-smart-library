Institutional Overview / LLRB

Dedicated to National Security Excellence & Learning.

The Library & Learning Resources Branch (LLRB) functions as the intellectual powerhouse of Rashtriya Raksha University (RRU), an Institution of National Importance under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

The branch caters to faculty, doctoral scholars, defense researchers, student cadets, and in-service security personnel by housing cutting-edge multidisciplinary collections and deploying modern digital library systems.

🛡️
Internal Security & Policing

Comprehensive policing doctrines, border guarding, and counter-insurgency studies.

💻
Cyber Security & Forensics

Threat intelligence, incident investigation, hardware forensics, and AI defense.

⚖️
Criminal Law & Criminology

Penal codes, juvenile justice, victimology, and constitutional jurisprudence.

⚓
Maritime & Coastal Defense

Maritime security, law of the sea, port security, and coastal surveillance.

Modern Library Infrastructure

Core Technological Assets

🔍

Koha Integrated LMS

Automated classification using Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC) and fully searchable online public catalog (Web OPAC).

🌐

MyLOFT Remote Access

Secure off-campus proxy access enabling students, scholars, and officers to access e-journals from anywhere in the world.

🛡️

Anti-Plagiarism (URKUND / Ouriginal)

Ensuring highest research integrity for Master's dissertations, doctoral theses, and security policy publications.

🏛️

National Digital Library (NDLI) Club

Partnered with Ministry of Education NDLI network providing access to millions of inter-institutional e-resources.

Wings & Facilities

Operational Sections & Patron Services

Providing streamlined access, academic assistance, and quiet spaces designed for deep study and strategic collaboration.

📥 Acquisition Section

Manages collection development, book suggestions from schools of study, defense monograph procurements, and vendor coordinating.

🏷️ Technical Processing Section

Handles cataloging, DDC classification, barcode tagging, and database indexing into the Koha-LMS system.

🔄 Automated Circulation Desk

Automated issue, return, renewal, fine management, and reservation holds for students, faculty, and research fellows.

📖 Reference & Serials Wing

Print periodicals, national newspapers, defense reviews, encyclopedias, and hands-on librarian reference assistance.

💻 Digital Library & Cyber Lab

Equipped with high-speed computing workstations, dedicated research terminals, high-speed Wi-Fi, and electronic database access.

🤝 Inter-Library Loan (ILL)

Facilitates document delivery and inter-institutional borrowing with premier security institutes and university networks across India.
